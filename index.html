Free RC Check - How to Check Vehicle Owner Name by Number Plate?

Orbittroniz RC Checker allows you to check vehicle owner name by entering number plate like MH01AB1234, DL01AB1234, KA01AB1234. Our AI fetches data from Vahan - Ministry of Road Transport, Govt of India. You get owner name (masked for privacy), vehicle model, registration date, insurance validity till date, PUC validity, finance/hypothecation status, fuel type, engine number, chassis number. This is useful for second hand vehicle buyers to verify if vehicle has loan, theft record, or fake RC.

Check Pending Challan by Vehicle Number - E-Challan Status

Enter your vehicle number in Challan Checker tool. We check pending e-challans from traffic police database. If challan found, we show challan amount, date, location, reason (overspeed, no helmet, no PUC). Pay challan before fine doubles. If no challan, you get green verified badge with 'No Pending Challans'.

Toll Calculator Mumbai Pune, Delhi Jaipur - FASTag Saving

Our Toll Calculator calculates total toll for route Mumbai to Pune (₹375 for truck), Delhi to Jaipur, Bangalore to Chennai. Enter from and to city, select vehicle type (LCV, Car, Bus), get total toll, distance, FASTag saving, toll plaza names. Save 10 minutes per plaza with FASTag.
